## 1. Context & Repository Tracking

To prevent loss of previous structural design parameters, technical specifications, and subsystem dimensions, all architectural updates are cross-referenced with the primary GitHub repository tree (**srctac-d/LSI-ACES**).

### Primary System Baselines Preserved:
1. **Structural Timber & Metal Framing:**
   * **Floor Joists:** Standardized to **2x6 dimensional profiles** (1.5 in x 5.5 in actual cross-section), allowing implementation in either VPI fire-retardant treated timber or Al-Li 2195 structural C-channels.
   * **18-Point Polygonal Arch Ribs:** 1.5 in x 3.5 in x 0.125 in structural tubing for primary hull load distribution.
   * **12 o'clock Overhead Spine:** Heavy-duty 4-inch S-beam (S4x2.64) functioning as continuous tension tie and 2,000 lb overhead monorail.

2. **Module Geometry & Clearances:**
   * **Hab / Optical Receiver Modules (01 & 02):** 12.0 ft OD, 11.3 ft clear inner floor chord.
   * **Garage & Utility Module:** 14.0 ft OD for rover maintenance and heavy equipment storage.
   * **Sub-Floor Basement:** ~3.8 ft clearance beneath the 2x6 floor joist grid for ECLSS, hydronics, power distribution, and thermal storage.

3. **In-Situ Construction & Pass-Through Interfaces:**
   * **Crew Bulkhead Hatches:** 34 in x 72 in walk-through hatches rated for +/- 7.0 psi bi-directional pressure containment.
   * **Modular Deck Panels:** 4 ft x 6 ft x 1 in basalt-fiber geocrete "Terry-cloth" 3D loop panels designed to fit through crew hatches.
   * **Garage Bay Doors:** Large-format (8x8 ft to 10x10 ft) cast geocrete doors with dual active inflatable fluorosilicone seals and 16 active shear dogs.

4. **Power & Thermal Integration:**
   * **Optical Receiver (Module 02):** 12-inch fused silica window accepting concentrated light from the 100 ft Lunar Power Tower, split 70% to CPV/Stirling conversion and 30% to sub-floor thermal storage.
