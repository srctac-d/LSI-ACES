## [0.2.0] - Integrated System & Fabrication Update

## [1.1.0] - 2026-07-28

### Added
* **Cable-Drawn Yarder Network:** Integrated fixed hold-fast anchors and reciprocating winch operations to offset 1/6th $g$ traction limits during site excavation.
* **Harmonic Cable De-Dusting:** Added passive/active cable vibration via cutter-teeth frequency synchronization and sheave gearing to remove abrasive regolith dust.
* **H2 Gas Seal Barrier Purge:** Included low-pressure Hydrogen (+0.1 to +0.5 PSI) micro-charge bleeding on winches and suit-ports to protect internal packings in hard vacuum.
* **Thermal & Shielding Insulation:** Detailed protective foam/insulation and regolith coverage mechanics.
