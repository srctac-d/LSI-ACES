1. SITE PREPARATION & HEAVY MOBILITY ASSETS

1.1 ACTER Civil Engineering Rover & Attachment Suite
Primary Mobility Architecture: Dedicated heavy-duty rover frame engineered to operate under low-gravity (1/6th g) traction constraints.

Surface Tool Heads & Regolith Attachments:
* Front-mounted scraping apron and dynamic grader blade for trenching and pad leveling.
* Deep-soil rotary drill head for pre-drill geotechnical anchoring analysis.
* Powered dynamic cutter bucket teeth for high-efficiency regolith breakage and active excavation.
* Sintering array / compaction rollers for surface stabilization.

Traction Deficit Workaround & Cable-Drawn Yarder Operations (Fixed Hold-Fast Network):
* To negate wheel slippage during high-load scraping and trenching, a stationary Hold-Fast Anchor System is driven into bedrock or deep consolidated substrate using expanding rock bolts or helical augers.
* The rover utilizes an internal, high-torque winch tug drum to crawl along high-tensile winch lines, trading vehicle mass for pure mechanical pulling power.

Cable-Drawn Yarder, Harmonic Cable Vibration & H2 Gas Seal Protection:
* Multiple Hold-Fast anchors are easily placed across the site to establish a cable-drawn yarder network.
* High-tensile winches pull a reciprocating scraper/bucket directly between anchor points over the excavation field.
* Harmonic De-Dusting: The oscillating frequency of the bucket's powered cutter teeth is synchronized with cable tension, or passed through a profiled gear-mesh sheave wheel, to actively vibrate high-tensile cables under load. This shakes caked abrasive dust off the wire rope before reaching winches without requiring dedicated wear parts.
* H2 Gas Barrier Seals: All winch bearing races, rotary seals, and actuator housings utilize a low-pressure Hydrogen (H2) gas charge bleed (+0.1 to +0.5 PSI) supplied from ISRU volatile storage. This outward gas barrier prevents backside dust migration into internal mechanical packings under hard vacuum.
* Eliminates Rover Risk: Deposits protective shielding over deployed habitat shells safely from a distance, completely eliminating the need for heavy mobile rovers to drive over un-compacted soil or expanded structures, removing rollover risks, reducing rover drivetrain dust wear, and lowering operational power draw.

3. PRESSURIZED MODULE ARCHITECTURE & LAYOUT MATRIX

3.0 Structural Architecture & Definitions: Circularized Flattened Truss (CFT) Assembly
An interlocking, low-profile arc-truss configuration composed of short, sequential triangular segments designed to follow the inner radius of the primary hull shell.

* Keystone Load-Tightening Action: Vertical and live deck loads drive adjacent segment wedge interfaces into direct compression, increasing overall structural rigidity as payload increases.
* Dual Tension-Compression Webbing: Triangular members alternate load distribution between axial tension and compression, eliminating point-load concentration across individual nodes.
* Structural Cohesion: Fastened perimeter radial nodes tie short individual segments into a single cohesive ring assembly, delivering high moment of inertia (E I) within a shallow vertical depth profile.

Every module shares a standardized Raised Joist Floor Profile, installed approximately 4 feet above the lower structural hull curve. This creates a continuous, protected Under-Floor Utility Basement housing high-voltage lines, thermal fluid loops, and atmospheric gas conduits completely isolated from primary living/working areas.
